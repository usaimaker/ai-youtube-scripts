# AI Video Generator: Create Videos in Minutes

Hook: Spending 3 hours editing one video? An AI video generator can turn a plain script into a finished clip in minutes — here is exactly how to do it.

## Why This Matters
- Faceless channels live or die by output speed. The faster you publish, the faster the algorithm learns who to show your videos to.
- Traditional editing eats up to 70 percent of a creator's time. AI video generators flip that ratio so you spend minutes, not hours.
- You need no camera, no voice actor, and no editing suite. Text in, video out — ideal for automation-first creators.

## How to Create Videos with AI (Step by Step)
1. Write a tight script of 150 to 300 words using a simple hook, problem, solution structure.
2. Paste it into an AI video generator and pick a voice, visual style, and aspect ratio (9:16 for Shorts, 16:9 for long-form).
3. Let the tool auto-generate scenes, stock visuals, captions, and a narrated voiceover.
4. Review the draft, trim dead air, and swap any weak clip for a stronger stock shot.
5. Export and upload with a keyword-first title plus a 3-line pinned comment that drives viewers to your next video.
