# Free AI Bookkeeping Stack: Kill Your Receipt Backlog

Hook: Your shoebox of receipts is quietly stealing a full workday every month.

## Why this matters

Small business owners lose hours typing receipts into spreadsheets. One sole proprietor cut monthly bookkeeping from six hours to forty-five minutes. Tool cost was zero.

## 1. Wave Starter

Wave Starter is genuinely free, forever. You get unlimited invoices, unlimited customers, and bank feed connections. Basic categorization and reporting are included. Receipt OCR sits in the paid tier at nineteen dollars monthly. For micro businesses, start free and upgrade only if volume demands it.

## 2. Gemini inside Google Docs

Photograph a receipt with your phone. Paste the image into a Google Doc. Gemini reads the date, amount, and merchant name. Copy that text into a spreadsheet. Manual typing disappears. This uses your existing free Google account, so there is nothing new to buy.

## 3. Google Sheets GEMINI function

Add the GEMINI function to your expense sheet. It suggests categories automatically. Coffee shop charges become meeting expenses. Printer paper becomes office supplies. It also flags odd entries, so duplicate or inflated amounts surface before month end instead of during tax season.

## 4. Zoho Books free plan

Zoho Books offers a real free tier for businesses under fifty thousand dollars annual revenue. Its Zia assistant handles categorization, payment reminders, and anomaly detection. You can ask plain English questions about your numbers. It feels like real software, not a patched together spreadsheet.

## 5. Self hosted n8n

n8n is open source and free when you host it yourself. Chain the pieces together. A receipt arrives in Gmail. It lands in your sheet. Your dashboard updates. You get a notification. Set it once, then check weekly instead of daily.

## Automation tip

Do not automate the whole month at once. Automate one step, the receipt intake, and run it for two weeks. Reported accuracy gains only appear when the input step is clean. Fix intake first, then chain reconciliation and reporting on top.
