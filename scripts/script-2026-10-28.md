# AI Voice Generator: Clone Any Voice in Minutes
Hook: What if you could turn any script into a natural, human-sounding voice in under a minute — no microphone, no studio, no voice actor?

## Why This Matters
Voice is the fastest-growing content format, but recording is slow, expensive, and exhausting. AI voice generators let you produce audiobooks, YouTube narrations, ads, and podcasts at scale in dozens of languages. The barrier to entry is now zero.

## 6 AI Voice Generators Worth Using
1. ElevenLabs — the gold standard for realistic, expressive voices and instant voice cloning.
2. Murf AI — clean studio-quality voices built for business presentations and ads.
3. PlayHT — huge multilingual library with low-latency API access.
4. Speechify — turn any text or doc into speech; great for repurposing content.
5. Descript Overdub — clone your own voice and edit audio by editing text.
6. CapCut / TikTok voices — free, fast, perfect for short-form social clips.

## How to Pick the Right One
Match the tool to the job: use ElevenLabs or Murf for hero content, free options for bulk shorts. Always listen to a sample in your target language before committing to a paid plan.

## Automation Tip
Chain it: write your script once, run it through an AI voice API, and auto-render the video with captions. A single no-code workflow can publish a narrated short every day on autopilot.
