Kokoro is an 82-million-parameter model released under Apache 2.0.

Your text never leaves your machine, so it stays fully private.

Feed it a three-second clip and it clones your voice instantly.

Then it speaks in six languages you may not even know.

Perfect for creators who want their own voice in foreign markets.
