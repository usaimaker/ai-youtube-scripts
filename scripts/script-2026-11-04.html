Why this matters

Email is where deals stall and time leaks. Manual triage, follow-ups, and tagging eat 5+ hours a week for most founders. AI flips email from a chore into a self-running system that sorts, replies, and routes while you sleep.

5 workflows you can automate today

1. Smart inbox sorting — AI labels, prioritizes, and routes messages to the right folder or person automatically.

2 Auto follow-ups — Set it once; the agent chases leads and clients until they reply, then stops on its own.

3. Draft replies — Generate on-brand responses from your history and tone; you just hit approve.

4. Meeting scheduler — Natural-language booking that reads your calendar and confirms without back-and-forth.

5. Newsletter segmentation — Tag subscribers by behavior and send the right email to the right group.

How to pick a tool

Start with what you already use (Gmail, Outlook). Add a rule-based AI agent before paying for a heavyweight platform. Test one workflow for a week, measure hours saved, then expand.

Automation tip

Chain it: connect your email agent to a no-code automation tool so a new lead triggers a welcome email, a CRM entry, and a Slack alert — all hands-free.
