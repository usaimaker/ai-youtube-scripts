# AI Website Builder: Launch a Site in Minutes
Hook: Want a real, working website without touching a line of code or hiring a developer? AI website builders can spin up a full site -- copy, images, and layout -- in minutes. Here is exactly how they work and which one to pick.

## Why This Matters
- The AI website builder market is booming: tools like Durable, Wix AI, and Hostinger now launch sites in under a minute, and demand keeps climbing every quarter.
- The old way (brief a developer, wait weeks, pay thousands) is dead for small business, side hustles, and landing pages.
- You stay in control: every AI-generated site is fully editable, so you tweak copy and branding without starting over.

## 5 AI Website Builders Worth Trying
1. Durable -- Generates a complete site (name, copy, images, contact form) in about 30 seconds; free trial, ideal for local businesses and side hustles.
2. Wix AI -- Mature builder that asks a few questions, then drafts sections, layout, and text you refine with prompts.
3. Hostinger AI Builder -- Budget all-in-one hosting plus AI design; a top pick for affiliate sites and small companies.
4. 10Web AI -- Recreates any existing site into a WordPress AI site, or builds new from a prompt with AI-written copy.
5. Framer AI -- Turns a plain-language prompt into a polished, modern, design-grade marketing site.

## How to Pick the Right One
- Selling products? Use Shopify Magic or Hostinger ecommerce for built-in store and checkout.
- Local biz or side hustle? Durable or Hostinger win on speed and price.
- Want a designer look? Framer AI leads on polish.
- Need WordPress power? 10Web is your move.
- Pro tip: always own your domain and check the platform lets you export -- never lock yourself into a builder you cannot leave.

## Automation Tip
Keep a swipe file of winning niches and a CSV of business details (name, services, city, phone). Wire a Make or Zapier flow so a new client form submission auto-drafts a starter site, then you just polish the copy. Pair it with an AI copy generator to fill every section in one click -- you ship, you never start from a blank page.
