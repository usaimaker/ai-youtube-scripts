Why this matters

Most "free" resume builders mean free to build, not free to download. The usable file sits behind a paywall. Free to build and free to export are two very different promises.

1. Know the trap before you start

Zety, Resume.io and Resume Genius all let you build at no cost. Their free export is a plain text file. A formatted PDF needs a cheap trial, near two or three dollars. That trial then renews around twenty five to thirty dollars every four weeks. Note "four weeks," not a month. That is thirteen charges a year, not twelve. Enhancv has no permanent free tier, and trial exports carry its branding.

2. OpenResume: the zero catch pick

OpenResume is open source and free forever. No sign up, no account, no export paywall. Your data stays inside your own browser. It follows US resume conventions and targets Greenhouse and Lever.

3. Rezi and Teal: when you want AI inside

Rezi's free plan gives you three PDF downloads. Teal lets you build unlimited resumes and export a clean PDF. But its best AI and keyword tools are credit throttled. Spend those free credits on your most important application.

4. FlowCV: one polished resume, no watermark

FlowCV's free tier covers a single resume. The PDF exports without any watermark. That is enough when you apply with one strong document.

5. Jobscan: check the match, not the looks

Jobscan is a scanner, not a builder. The free plan gives five scans each month. Paste your resume and the job post, and it scores keyword overlap. Save those five scans for jobs you actually want.

Automation tip

AI writing is the exact feature builders throttle hardest. So split the job in two. Ask a free chatbot to rewrite your bullets with strong verbs and real numbers. Then paste that text into a free, ATS safe builder. Always keep one column. Two column templates can confuse parsers like Workday, and your details get scrambled.
