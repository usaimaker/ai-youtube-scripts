Why this matters

- Manual transcription eats 4 to 6 hours per hour of audio; AI does it in minutes.

- Real use cases: meetings, podcasts, interviews, lectures, YouTube captions, court records.

- Whisper-class models now hit 95% plus accuracy across dozens of languages.

5 Best AI Transcription Tools

1. Whisper (OpenAI) - open-source, runs locally for free, multilingual, near-human accuracy. Best for privacy.

2. Otter.ai - live meeting notes, Zoom and Google Meet integration, searchable transcripts, free tier included.

3. AssemblyAI - API-first, automatic speaker labels, summarization, perfect for builders.

4. MacWhisper and TurboScribe - one-click desktop apps that batch-process files and videos.

5. Google Gemini and NotebookLM - upload long audio and get transcript plus instant summary.

How to pick

- Want free and 100 percent private? Run Whisper on your own machine.

- Need live meeting notes? Otter.ai.

- Building a product? AssemblyAI API.

Automation tip

Wire transcription into an n8n workflow: drop audio in a folder, trigger the Whisper API, save the transcript to Notion and email a summary. Zero manual steps, runs while you sleep.
