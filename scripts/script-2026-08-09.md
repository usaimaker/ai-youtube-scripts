# Free AI Note Taking Apps For Students (No Subscription)

Hook: Your lecture runs fifty minutes, but most free AI note takers stop recording at thirty.

## Why this matters

Students get pushed into subscriptions by tiny free tiers, not by better features.
A thirty minute cap dies halfway through one class.
These five tools survive a real week of lectures, and every one has a free path.
Quick note first: check your school policy before recording any lecture.

## 1. Otter, and the cap nobody mentions

Otter records live and transcribes in real time, which students love.
The free plan gives three hundred minutes a month.
But it also caps each single recording at thirty minutes.
Most lectures are longer, so you lose the ending.
Use Otter for short seminars and tutorials, not two hour lectures.

## 2. NotebookLM, the strongest free study brain

NotebookLM is free with a Google account and has no monthly minute meter.
It does not record live, so feed it what you already have.
Upload slides, PDFs, readings, and your lecture transcript.
It answers only from your sources, so it invents far less.
Then it builds a study guide, a mind map, and a podcast style audio overview.

## 3. Knowt, free flashcards without the paywall

Passive rereading feels productive and rarely works.
Knowt turns your notes or a PDF into flashcards and practice quizzes.
Flashcards stay free, which is where most study apps start charging.
Pair it with NotebookLM: synthesise there, then test yourself here.

## 4. Tactiq, for online lectures

Tactiq is a browser extension that reads captions from Google Meet and Zoom.
No bot joins the call, and nothing gets uploaded from your microphone.
The free tier covers about ten transcripts a month.
That is enough for a semester of online seminars.

## 5. Local Whisper, unlimited and offline

Every tool above has a limit. Whisper on your own laptop does not.
It is free, open source, and runs without internet.
Record the class on your phone, then transcribe the file at home.
Audio never leaves your machine, which matters for private tutorials.
Slower than cloud tools, but there is no meter and no monthly reset.

## Automation tip

Make one folder called Lectures on your laptop.
Set a nightly scheduled task that runs Whisper on any new audio file.
Every morning your transcripts are waiting, already named by date.
Drop them into a NotebookLM notebook per course, and revision builds itself.
