Hook: Starting a podcast used to mean microphones, editing software, and hours of post-production. Now an AI podcast generator can turn a script, article, or idea into a finished episode with realistic voices - here is how to launch one today.

Why this matters

- Podcasts build trust and authority faster than most other content formats.

- Editing and production used to block most would-be creators.

- AI voices and auto-editing remove the gear and skill barrier completely.

- Repurposing blog posts into audio captures a whole new audience.

- Consistent episodes compound - automation lets you publish on schedule.

5 AI podcast generators worth trying

1. ElevenLabs - studio-quality text-to-speech plus an AI hosts mode for natural dialogue.

2. NotebookLM - turn documents into a two-host audio overview in one click.

3. Adobe Podcast and Spotify AI Voice - clean up audio and generate voice clones.

4. Podcastle or Murf - full editing suites with AI voices and transcription.

5. Descript - script-to-episode with overdub and video podcast options.

How to pick the right one

- Decide your format first: solo narration, two-host show, or repurposed articles.

- Match voice quality to your brand - listen to samples before committing.

- Check export options: you need clean MP3 or WAV plus show notes for distribution.

- Start free, ship three episodes, then upgrade only if the workflow fits.

Automation tip

Wire it together: drop a new blog URL into a folder, trigger an n8n or Zapier flow that sends the text to NotebookLM or ElevenLabs, generates the episode and show notes, then auto-posts to your host such as Buzzsprout or Spotify and pings your Slack - a full episode with zero manual steps.
