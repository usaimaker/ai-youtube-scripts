Otter's free plan transcribes 300 minutes a month. That covers four weekly client calls comfortably. The real win is the automatic action-point summary. You stay present in the call instead of scribbling.

Descript's free tier gives you transcription-based video editing. Cut filler words automatically. Turn one 20-minute webinar into four social clips. No traditional editing skills required.
