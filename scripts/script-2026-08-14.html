Why this matters

Most "free" AI slide makers trap you at export. Some stamp a watermark; others lock clean PPTX behind paid plans. Knowing the real free limits saves you from redoing work before a meeting.

1. Canva Magic Design — the safe free default

Canva's free plan exports PowerPoint and PDF with no watermark on standard elements. The AI "Magic Design" is capped at a few uses, but the template library covers the rest. This is the one tool I'd open first.

2. Gamma — gorgeous, but watermarked free tier

Gamma makes the prettiest decks fastest. Its free plan gives about 400 one-time AI credits, then stops refilling. Free exports carry a "Made with Gamma" badge, and clean PPTX export sits behind the paid Plus plan.

3. SlidesAI — best if you live in Google Slides

SlidesAI runs inside Google Slides as an add-on. The free tier is rationed to a few decks a month, but exports stay watermark-free. Great when your whole workflow is already Google.

4. Beautiful.ai — the trap to avoid

Beautiful.ai has no real free plan — only a 14-day trial. After it ends, downloads stop entirely. If a tool won't let you keep your file for free, it isn't free.

5. The 100% free, zero-watermark hack

Ask ChatGPT or Gemini to write your slides as Markdown. Paste into Google Slides or convert to PowerPoint. Unlimited, no watermark, no sign-up. It beats any credit cap.

Automation tip

Keep a saved prompt: 'Turn this bullet list into a 10-slide Markdown deck.' Reuse it for every talk — generate, paste, present, done.
