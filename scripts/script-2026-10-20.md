# AI Automation Tools That Run Your Work on Autopilot

Hook: Stop doing the same busywork by hand — these AI automation tools watch your inbox, post your content, and sort your files while you sleep, and most of them are free to start.

## Why this matters
Everyone talks about AI like it is a chatbot you chat with. The real win is automation: connecting tools so tasks run themselves. The gap between "I tried ChatGPT once" and "my business runs on autopilot" is just a few free connectors. You do not need a developer or a big budget to close it.

## 1. Zapier for no-code triggers
Connect two apps and Zapier moves data between them on a trigger — a new lead in a form emails your CRM, a new order posts to Slack. Start on the free plan; the bottleneck is your imagination, not the tool.

## 2. Make for visual flows
When you outgrow linear zaps, Make lets you branch, filter, and loop. Build a visual scenario that grabs a feed, rewrites it, and schedules posts. Same $0 entry point, far more power.

## 3. n8n for self-hosted control
Want no monthly fees and full data ownership? n8n runs on your machine or a free cloud tier. Same agent-and-tool pattern, but you own the keys.

## 4. Browser agents for clicks
Tools like browser bots click through websites for you — filling forms, pulling prices, monitoring pages. The tedious stuff finally disappears.

## Automation tip
Stack the tools: a Make scenario grabs fresh content, n8n rewrites and schedules it, and a browser agent publishes it. Chain three free tools and you get a content machine that runs daily with zero manual work.
