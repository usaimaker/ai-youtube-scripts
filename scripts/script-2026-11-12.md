## 5 AI thumbnail makers worth trying
1. Canva Magic Design — start from a photo and get layout suggestions plus one-click backgrounds.
2. Thumbnail.AI and Fotor — built for YouTube sizes with click-worthy text presets.
3. Midjourney or DALL·E — generate unique hero images you can't find in stock.
4. Remove.bg with Photoshop Express — clean cutouts and quick composites on mobile.
5. CapCut text effects — bold, high-contrast titles that stay readable on a phone.

## How to pick the right one
- Match the tool to your workflow: generate art with AI, assemble fast with Canva.
- Export at 1280x720 and keep text legible at small size.
- Test two variants per video and keep the winner for your next upload.

## Automation tip
Batch your thumbnails: generate a month of drafts in one session, then schedule uploads with a tool like n8n or Zapier so publishing never stalls waiting on artwork.
