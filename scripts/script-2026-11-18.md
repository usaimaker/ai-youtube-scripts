# AI Social Media Manager: Automate Your Posts

Hook: Posting daily feels like a second job. An AI social media manager writes captions, schedules posts and drafts replies - your brand stays visible while you work.

## Why This Matters
Every platform wants fresh content. Doing it manually burns hours. An AI social media manager learns your tone, turns one idea into posts for each channel and keeps a steady rhythm: consistent on-brand content, fewer errors, no daily scramble.

## 5 Things an AI Social Media Manager Does Better
1. Write in your voice - Learn tone, hashtags and length from sample posts.
2. Repurpose one idea - Turn a video into a LinkedIn thread, Instagram caption, post and email.
3. Pick the best time - Study your audience and schedule for maximum reach.
4. Build a content calendar - Two-week plans mapped to themes and campaigns.
5. Draft replies - Answer common questions on brand and flag the rest for a human.

## How to Pick the Right One
Try Buffer, Hootsuite or Later with built-in AI writing. Check four things: does it learn your voice, cover all your platforms, allow review before publishing, and export your content? Avoid tools that publish without approval.

## Automation Tip
Drop a blog URL or voice note into a folder, let the AI draft platform captions, queue them for a quick review, then publish on schedule. One source, a week of content.
