# Free AI Voice Generator Like ElevenLabs (Commercial Use)

Hook: You want studio-quality voices for your videos, but ElevenLabs locks commercial rights behind a paywall.

## Why this matters
ElevenLabs' free plan gives you 10,000 characters a month. That's about ten minutes of audio.
Worse, you cannot use that audio commercially, and you must credit them.
Paid plans start at five dollars a month just to sell what you make.

## 1. TTSMaker - browser, free, commercial
Paste your script and download the voiceover right in your browser. No install needed.
The free plan grants full commercial rights, with no attribution required.
You get 20,000 characters weekly across 100+ languages and 600 voices.
Pick the "unlimited" voices to skip the weekly cap entirely.

## 2. Kokoro TTS - local, unlimited, private
Kokoro is an 82-million-parameter model released under Apache 2.0.
It is free for any use, including commercial projects, forever.
It runs on your own CPU in real time, with 54 voices in 8 languages.
Your text never leaves your machine, so it stays fully private.

## 3. OpenVoice - clone your own voice free
OpenVoice is MIT-licensed and free for commercial use.
Feed it a three-second clip and it clones your voice instantly.
Then it speaks in six languages you may not even know.
Perfect for creators who want their own voice in foreign markets.

## Automation tip
Batch your scripts into one folder and run Kokoro overnight on a CPU.
Wake up to a folder of finished MP3 narrations at zero per-character cost.
